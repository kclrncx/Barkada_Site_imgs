About Us

Welcome to the CTU Daanbantayan Student Services and Information Center.
This website is a student-focused portal designed to provide convenient
access to commonly needed academic information and student services.

The purpose of this portal is to organize important student information
in one accessible location. Through this website, students can view their
academic schedules, subjects, student information, and available services.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to make important student information and services
easier to access through one convenient and organized online portal.
This website helps students quickly find academic schedules, student
information, document services, and other resources they may need.

What We Offer

- Access to students academic schedules
- Student documents request service
- Student uniform orders
- Information and Academic details

For Students

This portal is designed with students in mind. Whether you need to
check your class schedule, review your subjects, request a document,
or find information about student services, the portal provides a
central place to begin.

We hope this project demonstrates how a simple and well-organized
website can make commonly needed student information easier to access.

Our Goal

Our goal is to create a user-friendly student information portal that keeps important academic information organized, accessible, and easy to understand.

This project is intended as an educational web development project demonstrating the use of HTML to create a functional and organized student services website.
